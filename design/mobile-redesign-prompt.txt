Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high fidelity mobile product redesign concept board for Pantry Scoop.
Primary request: Reimagine the best possible mobile design and UX for this real personal pantry and recipe application. Produce a beautifully resolved, credible, implementation-ready visual concept with FOUR full-height mobile screen interfaces side by side, each designed for a 390x844 phone viewport. Wide high resolution presentation board, straight-on flat UI, no perspective distortion, no device hardware, no ornamental surroundings, large enough for legible actual UI. Small presentation labels above each screen: "01 / PANTRY", "02 / SCAN REVIEW", "03 / RECIPES", "04 / MY KITCHEN". Screens aligned and equal sized; a narrow neutral warm backdrop separates them.

Product context: The app tracks ingredient names, categories, notes and binary in-stock/out-of-stock state; it does NOT track quantities or expiration dates. Users scan photos of pantry/fridge/grocery bags (up to six photos), manually add items, toggle stock in one tap, generate recipes based on their pantry, chosen dish, servings, appliances and ingredient-buy allowance, save recipes, and configure appliance limits and dietary defaults. Background scans and recipe jobs persist when leaving the app. Use ONLY this model, except a proposed review-before-save step for scans. Avoid fake expiry dates, waste metrics, calorie dashboards or shopping checkout.

Art direction: contemporary, quietly distinctive kitchen companion with outstanding information hierarchy. Existing brand "Pantry Scoop." with tiny thin-stroke pantry jar mark. Warm ivory backgrounds (#f8f6ef), rich dark forest olive (#314b36) primary controls, charcoal text, restrained citrus accent, muted sage for selected chips. Elegant serif ONLY for wordmark; practical refined sans-serif for all product headings, labels and rows. Crisp typography, strong contrast, disciplined 8px spacing, fine borders, subtle soft corners, minimal elevation, tasteful generous breathing room balanced with efficient density. Small monochrome consistent line icons. No emoji, no oversized marketing headings, no decorative hero imagery, no slogans, no gradients, no giant empty areas, no glassmorphism. Food photography ONLY as small meaningful recipe result thumbnails. Thumb-friendly 44-48px minimum targets, readable 16px interface controls. Show subtle mobile status bar and safe-area home indicator. All four screens feel like one coherent real app.

Shared navigation: fixed bottom navigation on screens 1,3,4 with THREE labeled tabs: "Pantry", "Recipes", "Kitchen". Show selected tab with understated sage background and forest icon. Upper right small account avatar. A primary action belongs just above the bottom navigation, within thumb reach, without covering content. Clear visual distinction between selected state and action.

Screen 1 Pantry, primary daily task: access ingredients immediately. Compact brand header. Heading "My pantry", below "24 in stock · 4 to restock". Visible full-width search field "Search ingredients". Horizontal segmented status filters "In stock 24" selected and "To restock 4", plus compact "All categories" filter. Ingredient list in category groups with slim section labels "Produce" and "Dairy & eggs", realistic rows: "Cherry tomatoes" note "Half a punnet", "Baby spinach", "Lemons", "Eggs" note "Bottom shelf", "Greek yogurt", "Parmesan". Each row has a clearly tappable green checked round stock control on left and an understated edit affordance on right. Useful density, not bloat. Compact "Cook with my pantry" text link. Bottom action row above navigation: large green camera action "Scan groceries" and secondary outlined plus "Add". No food photo on pantry.

Screen 2 Scan review, a focused transient full-screen workflow without bottom navigation: top back arrow, title "Review scan", a tiny row of 3 real grocery photo thumbnails, label "3 photos · Scan complete". Concise status panel "6 ingredients found" and supporting text "Review before adding to your pantry." Group "New ingredients 4" with checked selection controls for "Cherry tomatoes", "Baby spinach", "Greek yogurt", "Lemons"; each has small category text and unobtrusive edit pencil. Group "Back in stock 2" contains "Eggs" and "Parmesan". One muted expandable row "Already in your pantry · 3". Bottom sticky forest green CTA "Add 6 ingredients", secondary small link "Add another photo". Show checkboxes selected and clear count. This is a proposed UX improvement, visually a finished product screen.

Screen 3 Recipes, primary task get meal ideas quickly: brand header, title "What shall we cook?" with compact subtext "Made for your pantry." A clean compact input "What are you craving?" with example "Quick pasta, something spicy…". Side by side inputs "Dish" value "Dinner", "Servings" value "2". Single collapsed disclosure row "More options" with summary "Pantry only · 3 ideas", showing chevron. Full-width primary button "Find recipe ideas" with tiny spark line icon. Then section "Ideas for you", two compact recipe cards: "Lemon & spinach pasta" with small warm natural pasta thumbnail, metadata "20 min · 2 servings", sage label "Uses your pantry", bookmark outline; "Tomato & egg skillet" with tiny skillet food thumbnail, metadata "15 min · 2 servings", same pantry label and bookmark. Small secondary segment or link for "Saved 8". Keep enough room for bottom nav, no photo hero, no fictional recipe rankings.

Screen 4 Kitchen, primary task set cooking constraints: brand header, title "My kitchen". Small section "Appliances" and understated plus control "Add". Two clean appliance cards with small line icons and edit affordance: "Air fryer", note "4 L basket"; "Ice cream maker", note "1.2 L bowl · Mix 700–850 ml". Section "Cooking defaults", rows "Servings" value "2", "Units" value "Metric", "Language" value "English". Section "Dietary preferences" with input value "Vegetarian, less sugar". Bottom full-width forest button "Save preferences" above nav. No appliance photography, no implementation vocabulary, no ChatGPT usage settings in kitchen.

Composition: put focus on the screen UI. The visual itself must communicate daily task clarity and thoughtful touch UX. Correct, consistent, sharp text. Realistic complete UI, polished enough for a product design portfolio, restrained branding with highly usable controls. No extra screen panels or captions inside phone UI, no watermark.
