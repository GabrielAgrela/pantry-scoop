Privacy

Pantry Scoop · last updated 4 October 2026

Pantry Scoop is a small, private kitchen app that keeps track of what is in your pantry and suggests recipes. It is run by one person for themselves and invited friends.

What we keep

  • Your sign-in identity: name, email address and profile picture from ChatGPT or Google, and the account IDs those services give us. We use them only to sign you in and show who is signed in.
  • Your pantry: the ingredients, kitchen settings and saved recipes you add.
  • ChatGPT connection: if you sign in with ChatGPT and allow plan usage, the tokens that let Pantry Scoop use your ChatGPT plan. They are stored encrypted.

Google sign-in is used only to confirm who you are. Pantry Scoop does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Photos and AI

When you scan groceries or ask for recipes, the photos and your pantry list are sent to the AI you chose in your account menu: OpenAI (on your own ChatGPT plan) or DeepSeek. Photos are not stored on our server. Recent scan photos stay only in your own browser.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. It is shared only with the AI provider you chose, to answer your request.

Deleting your data

Use Delete account in the account menu to remove your account and everything in it. You can also disconnect ChatGPT or Google there, and remove Pantry Scoop from your ChatGPT or Google account settings at any time.
